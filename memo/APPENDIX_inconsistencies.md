# Appendix: errors, inconsistencies and open definitions across the Resilient Schools proposal

*Prepared from the district's own documents: the Aug. 25, 2026 proposal deck; the February 2024, 2025 and 2026 Annual Enrollment Trend Reports; the Oct. 21, 2025 work session; the Resilient Schools FAQ and bvsd.org pages; the Sept. 9, 2025 boundary study; and BVSD's October pupil-count files. Every item cites the page. Items are graded: **E** = the documents contradict each other or themselves; **U** = a figure the documents use but never define or reconcile; **N** = a figure that cannot be reproduced from the documents' own inputs; **P** = a process fact. Nothing here asserts a motive. The purpose is to show that the figures on which every component rests have not been reconciled, so that the Board can ask staff to reconcile them before acting on any of them. Section 2 lists what does check out.*

## 1. Findings

### C. Projection uncertainty that applies to every component

| # | Item | What the documents say | Grade |

The district's own record shows one two-year-ahead school-level projection in ten missing by more than 13% (59 and 29 cases from the Feb 2024 and Feb 2025 tables against the October counts), and revisions to four- and five-year targets of about 12% at the 80th percentile, with a maximum of 46%. | U |

## 2. What checks out (so the reader knows the audit was even-handed)
- Every post-change utilization percentage on pp. 25, 37, 39, 48, 51 and 54 reproduces from its own range and capacity to rounding.
- The Feb 2026 table's column totals (9,689; 9,408; 14,543) equal the sums of its rows; the advisory and engagement counts on p. 10 (7 + 2 for 2025-26; 6 + 5 for 2030-31) reproduce exactly from the table's rows at the stated thresholds; and the "14 schools at or below two classes per grade by 2030" on p. 11 reproduces.
- The deck's Boulder "current state" (63.1%, 2,585 open seats, p. 43) reproduces from the Feb 2026 table plus Horizons; its 2030 Boulder enrollment (4,241) is within 25 of the table's sum.
- The "capacity reduction 1,906" (p. 58) reproduces: Birch 418 + Flatirons 320 + Mesa 418 + Community Montessori building 369 + Monarch K-5 381.
- Mesa and Bear Creek's October counts, grade counts and prior-year figures reconcile exactly across all twelve years of pupil-count files, and CDE's membership agrees to within one or two students.

## 3. What the Board can ask staff to supply before acting on any component
1. One table, for every component: the share of the sending school's students assumed to follow, the resident capture rate assumed at each receiving school, the open-enrollment inflow assumed, and whether choice management is applied (A3, A4, B4).
2. The definition and reconciliation of the resident figures on p. 44 and p. 51, and of the Boulder resident trend on p. 43 against the area trends on pp. 25, 37, 48, 51 and 54 (A1, A2).
3. The room-by-room basis for each program-capacity change since January 2024, and the classroom footprint of AIM and RISE at each receiving school (B1, B5).
4. The inputs behind "67.9%" (p. 55) and "6" (p. 58) (B7, B8).
5. The kindergarten-intake assumption used in the January 2026 run for each school in the package, and the reason for the direction of Bear Creek's revision (A7).
6. By-school components of the $3.5–4.0M recurring savings and the $7.5–10.0M facility line; the bond offset; whether the $5.0M is incremental; and the carrying cost of the closed buildings (B9).
7. The final capacity of Monarch 6-8 (B3) and the adopted open-enrollment priority (B4), both before any component that depends on them is approved.

*Sources and reproducibility: every table above is computed in `analysis/08_district_claims.py`, `analysis/10_package_implied_shares.py` and `scripts/parse_ocr_capacity_tables.py` from the documents under `data/raw/`; the OCR'd capacity tables were checked row by row against the official October counts.*
