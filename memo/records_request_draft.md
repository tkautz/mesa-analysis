Under C.R.S. § 24-72-201 et seq., I request copies of the following records, in electronic form where they exist:

1. The worksheet, spreadsheet or model output that produces the merged-school enrollment ranges of 392–445 (2027-28) and 403–462 (2030-31) for Bear Creek on p. 51 of the Aug. 25, 2026 presentation, including: the projected resident students by grade and year for the combined attendance area (the 473 and 522 on p. 51); the assumed share of those residents attending Bear Creek; the assumed number of students open-enrolled from outside the combined area, from outside the district, and placed; and any cap or "managed choice" assumption applied to Bear Creek. 6. Any analysis of the K-2 / 3-5 reconfiguration of Mesa and Bear Creek referenced on p. 56, including its fiscal impact.
7. The number of Mesa-area addresses of enrolled students more than 1.5 miles from Bear Creek, and any transportation cost estimate for the combined area.
8. The "final plan for study" materials described for September 2026 in the June 9, 2026 Board update (p. 37: updated attendance boundaries, open enrollment preferences, analysis of facility improvements required), if they exist in draft.

If any item is withheld, please identify the record and the statutory basis. If fees exceed the statutory no-charge hour, please provide an estimate before proceeding.
