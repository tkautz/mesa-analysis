# Public comment, BVSD Board of Education, September 8, 2026 (two minutes, ~280 words)

Good evening. My name is Tim Kautz. I'm the parent of a kindergartner at Mesa Elementary. I'm also an economist who does program evaluation for a living, and I'm speaking tonight as a parent, for myself.

I'll concede the obvious: Mesa is small, and the district's projection that it stays small is reasonable. But closing a school can't be undone, and keeping one open can. An irreversible decision deserves a higher standard of evidence, and there are four things this Board has not been given.

First, a projection with a margin of error. The district's own numbers for these two schools have moved ten to sixteen percent from one year's run to the next; Bear Creek's 2029 figure went from 272 to 310 in a single year. No one has told you how wide the band is.

Second, the assumptions behind the merged-school range. The district's own projections for Mesa and Bear Creek add up to 521 students, more than the Bear Creek building holds. Its published range tops out at 462. The difference is an assumption about how many Mesa families leave, and it isn't stated.

Third, a plan that fits. Fitting the district's own data, there is roughly one chance in six that the merged school is over the building's capacity by 2030, and much higher if kindergarten stays where it has been for five years. That is before the RISE program moves in.

Fourth, a Mesa-specific cost figure. The deck has package totals only.

I am not asking you to reject the whole proposal. I am asking you to decline to approve this piece at this time, and to direct staff to come back with those four things before you close a school you can't reopen. Thank you.
