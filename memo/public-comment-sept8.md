# Public comment, BVSD Board of Education, September 8, 2026 (two minutes)

Good evening. My name is Tim Kautz. I'm the parent of a kindergartner at Mesa Elementary. I'm also an economist who evaluates programs for a living, but I'm speaking tonight as a parent.

I'll concede the obvious: Mesa is small, and the district's projection that it stays small is fair. But closing a school can't be undone, and keeping one open can. An irreversible decision deserves a higher standard of evidence, and there are four things this Board has not been given.

First, the assumption behind the merged-school range. The district's own projections for Mesa and Bear Creek add up to 521 students. The Bear Creek building holds 492. The proposal's range tops out at 462. The district's website says Bear Creek can house both schools, but no document shows the calculation. On its own resident numbers, today's attendance pattern gives about 550, and the FAQ says the choice and space plan will be written after your vote.

Second, a projection with a margin of error. Bear Creek's 2029 figure went from 272 to 310 in one year, and its range in the proposal is the widest of any receiving school.

Third, proof that the merged school fits. Using the district's own grade counts, I find one chance in six that the merged school is over capacity in 2030 if kindergarten keeps shrinking, and better than even if kindergarten holds where it has been. That is before RISE moves in and takes classrooms.

Fourth, a Mesa-specific cost figure. The deck has package totals only.

I am not asking you to reject the whole proposal. I am asking you to decline to approve this piece at this time, and to direct staff to come back with those four things before you close a school you can't reopen. One question for staff tonight: what capture rate and how many choice seats does 462 assume? Thank you.
