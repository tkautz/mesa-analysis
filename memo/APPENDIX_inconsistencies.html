Appendix: errors, inconsistencies and open definitions across the Resilient Schools proposal

Prepared from the district's own documents: the Aug. 25, 2026 proposal deck; the February 2024, 2025 and 2026 Annual Enrollment Trend Reports; the Oct. 21, 2025 work session; the Resilient Schools FAQ and bvsd.org pages; the Sept. 9, 2025 boundary study; and BVSD's October pupil-count files. Every item cites the page. Items are graded: E = the documents contradict each other or themselves; U = a figure the documents use but never define or reconcile; N = a figure that cannot be reproduced from the documents' own inputs; P = a process fact. Nothing here asserts a motive. The purpose is to show that the figures on which every component rests have not been reconciled, so that the Board can ask staff to reconcile them before acting on any of them. Section 2 lists what does check out.

1. Findings

B. Figures that are used but not defined or reconciled

# Item What the documents say Grade
B1 Program capacity was restated for seven schools across the three tables, without explanation. Feb 2024 → Feb 2025: Bear Creek 467 → 492 (+25); Coal Creek 516 → 492 (−24); Douglass 442 → 418 (−24). Feb 2025 → Feb 2026: Kohl 516 → 541 (+25); Aspen Creek 461 → 442 (−19); Community Montessori 392 → 369 (−23); Louisville 615 → 590 (−25). District total 14,608 → 14,585 → 14,543. The footnote in every table is "program capacity based on current use of the building"; no table says which rooms changed. Two of the schools whose capacity rose are the two receiving schools of the consolidations (Bear Creek +25, Kohl +25); one that fell is a school proposed for closure (Douglass −24). The utilization thresholds on which advisory status turns are computed on these denominators. U
B2 Two definitions of a "round." The tables convert enrollment to rounds at 150 students per round (Bear Creek 312 → 2.1; Mesa 224 → 1.5) but state capacity in rounds at a different divisor (492 = 3.5 rounds, i.e., 141 per round; 418 = 3.0, i.e., 139). At 150 per round the Bear Creek building is 3.3 rounds, not 3.5. U
B3 Monarch 6-8 capacity is undetermined. Deck p. 39: "Capacity: 890**"; "**Design will need to be completed to determine final capacity." The reconfiguration's utilization figures (63–67%, 55–58%) are computed on a capacity that does not yet exist. U
B4 The open-enrollment priority every range depends on is still under study. FAQ p. 2: "Staff presented a draft open enrollment priority to the Board for consideration … The Board will study this item further on Sept. 8." Every post-change range assumes the flows this rule will govern. P / U
B5 What 492 seats at Bear Creek contains. Deck p. 49: Mesa's RISE program moves to Bear Creek; bvsd.org page p. 7: "all existing AIM and RISE programs would be housed at Bear Creek." No document gives the classroom footprint of these programs or says whether 492 nets them out. U
B6 "10 schools needed" vs 13 remaining. Deck p. 43: Boulder has 16 PK-5 schools and needs 10 "to sustain three classes/grade." The package closes three Boulder schools, leaving 13. Whether a further round is intended is not stated. U
B7 Package headline "14 → 6 schools below two classes per grade" (p. 58). The "14" reproduces exactly from the Feb 2026 table (14 non-exempt schools projected below 300 in 2030-31). The "6" cannot be reproduced from the deck's own ranges: after the package, the schools projected below 300 in 2030-31 are Heatherwood (260–268), Creekside (215), Community Montessori (167) and Sanchez (298), with Eisenhower below 300 at the low end of its range (297–302): four or five, plus the three exempt mountain schools. The definition behind "6" is not given. N
B8 Post-change Boulder utilization "67.9%" (p. 55). The current figure (63.1%, 2,585 open seats) reproduces exactly from the Feb 2026 table plus Horizons (capacity 7,005, enrollment 4,420). Removing the capacity the package closes in Boulder (Flatirons 320, Mesa 418, Montessori building 369; Douglass's building is re-used by High Peaks) and using the deck's own 2030 enrollment (4,241) gives 71.9%; using current enrollment gives 74.9%. 67.9% is reached only if post-change Boulder enrollment is about 4,005, roughly 240 below the deck's own 2030 projection. What the figure assumes is not stated. N
B9 Cost figures. Deck p. 60: recurring savings $3.5–4.0M; facility modifications $7.5–10.0M, "some offset by savings from Bond projects" (amount not given); transition support $5.0M, "budgeted, FY27" (whether this is incremental to the plan is not stated). No per-school breakdown of either line for any component; no figure for maintaining or disposing of the four closed buildings ("Decisions about the future use of buildings are not part of the … proposal", FAQ p. 3); no portable, program-relocation or transportation cost. U
B10 Preschool locations after the vote. Deck p. 64 and FAQ p. 4: locations for 2027-28 "will be shared by Oct. 1"; the vote is September 22. Five of the affected schools' preschool families cannot know where their program will be when the decision is taken. P
B11 Transportation exposure. Deck p. 64 states the 1.5-mile eligibility rule but no count of addresses that cross it under any component. About 89% of the Mesa attendance area's land lies more than 1.5 miles from Bear Creek (BVSD ArcGIS polygons; area, not addresses). U

2. What checks out (so the reader knows the audit was even-handed)

  • Every post-change utilization percentage on pp. 25, 37, 39, 48, 51 and 54 reproduces from its own range and capacity to rounding.
  • The Feb 2026 table's column totals (9,689; 9,408; 14,543) equal the sums of its rows; the advisory and engagement counts on p. 10 (7 + 2 for 2025-26; 6 + 5 for 2030-31) reproduce exactly from the table's rows at the stated thresholds; and the "14 schools at or below two classes per grade by 2030" on p. 11 reproduces.
  • The deck's Boulder "current state" (63.1%, 2,585 open seats, p. 43) reproduces from the Feb 2026 table plus Horizons; its 2030 Boulder enrollment (4,241) is within 25 of the table's sum.
  • The "capacity reduction 1,906" (p. 58) reproduces: Birch 418 + Flatirons 320 + Mesa 418 + Community Montessori building 369 + Monarch K-5 381.
  • Mesa and Bear Creek's October counts, grade counts and prior-year figures reconcile exactly across all twelve years of pupil-count files, and CDE's membership agrees to within one or two students.

3. What the Board can ask staff to supply before acting on any component

  1. One table, for every component: the share of the sending school's students assumed to follow, the resident capture rate assumed at each receiving school, the open-enrollment inflow assumed, and whether choice management is applied (A3, A4, B4).
  2. The definition and reconciliation of the resident figures on p. 44 and p. 51, and of the Boulder resident trend on p. 43 against the area trends on pp. 25, 37, 48, 51 and 54 (A1, A2).
  3. The room-by-room basis for each program-capacity change since January 2024, and the classroom footprint of AIM and RISE at each receiving school (B1, B5).
  4. The inputs behind "67.9%" (p. 55) and "6" (p. 58) (B7, B8).
  5. The kindergarten-intake assumption used in the January 2026 run for each school in the package, and the reason for the direction of Bear Creek's revision (A7).
  6. By-school components of the $3.5–4.0M recurring savings and the $7.5–10.0M facility line; the bond offset; whether the $5.0M is incremental; and the carrying cost of the closed buildings (B9).
  7. The final capacity of Monarch 6-8 (B3) and the adopted open-enrollment priority (B4), both before any component that depends on them is approved.

Sources and reproducibility: every table above is computed in analysis/08_district_claims.py, analysis/10_package_implied_shares.py and scripts/parse_ocr_capacity_tables.py from the documents under data/raw/; the OCR'd capacity tables were checked row by row against the official October counts.
