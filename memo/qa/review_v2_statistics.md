# Adversarial statistics review, report v2 (report/report.tex)

Scope: Executive summary and Sections 1-6 against `analysis/output/table12,14,15,16,18,19,20_*.csv`, scripts 12/14/15/16/18/19/20, `data/clean/`. Every number drawn from those scripts reproduces except as listed. Recomputations in `scratchpad/recheck.py`.

No BLOCKER. Six MAJOR, seven MINOR.

## MAJOR

**1. Table `births`, row 4 is mislabeled.** Cell: "Births, 2014--25, $B_{2025}$ at 2022--24 mean: 576". Script 18 runs that sensitivity on the **2020--25** window (`("births spec, 2020-25, B2025 at 2022-24 mean", (2020, 2025), B_future_alt)`). As printed a reader infers the $B_{2025}$ change raises 546 to 576; it lowers 592 to 576. Fix: relabel "2020--25" or rerun on 2014--25.

**2. The births headline rests on the 2024--25 entry ratios (Section 4).** Sentence: "a births-based method ... does not lower the answer." Recomputed 2030--31 merged central (90% following, same seed and progression pool): window 2014--25 = 547 (P>492 83%); drop 2024 only = 519 (74%); drop 2024--25 = 506 (64%); fixed median ratio = 538; log-normal on log-ratio = 552; OLS $K = 44.7 + 0.63B$ (corr 0.65, elasticity 0.47) = 546. Functional form is not the issue; the window is: the two latest ratios (2.55, 1.69) carry about 40 students. The spec also implies a 2030 kindergarten of 90--98, above every intake since 2017 (max 84). Suggested headline: "Carrying the district's own births forward at the entry ratios observed since 2014 gives a 2030--31 central estimate of about 505--590 depending on which years' ratios are used (546 for the full window; about 505--520 if the two most recent, high ratios are excluded); in every variant the central estimate is at or above the level assumption's 503 and the top of the district's range. The method does not lower the answer; it does not make it precise."

**3. "Births ... ran 73--88 a year through 2014" is false (Section 4).** Four-area births 2005--2014: 88, 60, 81, 76, 83, 87, 62, 76, 71, 73 (`bvsd_births_by_attendance_area_wide.csv`). Fix: "60--88 a year in 2005--2014". Add "and 1.7 in 2025" after "2.5 times in 2024".

**4. The 23-sections headline is a ceiling artifact (Exec. summary item 3; Section 3; Table `sections`).** 24.58 is the budget book's "guideline ratio ... with adjustments made to accommodate individual grade levels" (budget_inputs_fy2027.csv, p.121), a staffing allocation, not a class-size cap. At that ratio the allocation is 473/24.58 = 19.2 FTE (2027 trend), 20.1 (level); "23" is `ceil(grade/24.58)` summed over six grades, about +0.6 section per grade (a 99-student grade becomes five classes of 20). The BVEA goals (26/29/31), which the district will cite, give 19--21, and C-6 permits combined-grade classes. Fix: item 3 should read "19--23 sections depending on the class-size basis, against 21 rooms before RISE and AIM"; Section 3 should print 19.2/20.1 FTE beside the 23.

**5. Placements are asserted to be from outside the area; the matrix does not say (Section 1: "121 students come from outside the area: ... 23 placements").** The by-area cells sum to enrollment minus out-of-district minus placements (Bear Creek 286 = 312 - 13 - 13; Mesa 204 = 224 - 10 - 10), so placed students' residence is unreported. Treating all 23 as outside is the assumption most favourable to the proposal (any resident among them raises capture above 83% and lowers manageable outside enrollment below 121), so the conclusion holds, but the sentence overstates the document. Fix: "23 placements, whose residence the matrix does not give (treated as outside, the assumption most favourable to the proposal)". Add that placements and out-of-district are not open enrollment: cutting outside enrollment to 30 means removing 91 of the 98 seats JECC-R governs.

**6. Contradiction on bias.** Section 2: "The district has leaned neither high nor low." Appendix D: "+1.1%, so the district has leaned slightly high." Recomputed +1.09%, year-clustered SE 0.48 (year means -0.5 to +3.7), 54% of projections above count. Fix Section 2: "leaned slightly high on average (+1%), driven by 2021--22".

## MINOR

**7. "one miss in ten exceeds 8%"** is the right reading of the pooled 90th percentile (8.16%; 11.6% of school-years above 8%), but the tail sits in 2021--22 (yearly p90 13.9% and 11.5%; other years 4.4--9.6%); without them 7.7%. The 328 school-years share year shocks (naive SE of the mean 0.28 vs 0.48 clustered), so say "one school-year in ten", not a per-school probability. The 2025 case (Jan 23 projection; Bear Creek 308, Mesa 230) is the Jan 2025 run already scored in Table `err`: the "two measures" overlap in one year. Horizons and dates (Feb--May; Jan 2025) are stated correctly.

**8. Conditional run (Section 2, Table `cond`) attributes nothing; readers will assume the K refit.** Recomputed: refitting the trend with K = 68 moves Bear Creek's slope from -0.039 to -0.035/yr (fitted 2027 K +1.4), Mesa's the other way; the level window moves 43.7 to 47.3 (BC). Nearly all of the +18/+20 comes from Bear Creek's August grades 1--5 sitting 22 above the model's expected 2026 (330 vs 308) and progressing at Bear Creek's >1 ratios; the August progression ratios entering the resampling pool add about 1 (2027) and 5 (2030). Fix: say so in one sentence, and add "August-to-October attrition is unknown". The "as if it were the October count" framing is adequate.

**9. JECC-R "one sixth per grade" (Table `newentrants`).** K outside share 16/76 = 21% vs 121/536 = 23%, so outside students sit slightly higher in the grades.
