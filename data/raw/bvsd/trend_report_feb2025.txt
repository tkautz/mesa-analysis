Projection Methodology
● Cohort survival method is the principal
technique (standard for most school
districts).
● Looks at the rate each cohort (or
class) progresses to the next level,
then applies past averages to current
enrollment.
● BVSD modifies to better account
resident student trends and adds
inputs for open enrollment flow, new
housing development, and birth rates.

===== trend_report_feb2025.pdf | page 8 of 12 =====
Projection Assumptions
● Open-enrollment flows and net migration are
relatively stable.
● New housing is constructed at a similar rate as
estimated in projection.
● Ratio of births to subsequent kindergarteners is relatively
stable.
● Primarily based on past trends, but births, new housing,
and open enrollment updates provide glimpses forward.
● Any future events that depart from current trends will
push projections in a different direction (COVID-19).

Enrollment Trend Summary
● For the 2024-25 school year, there are eight schools that are in the Enrollment Advisory Phase
(Coal Creek, Community Montessori, Eisenhower, Eldorado (K-5), Flatirons, Kohl, Mesa, and
Whittier) meaning that they are fewer than 2.0 rounds and 60% capacity.
● No additional schools were in the Community Engagement Phase in 2024, with fewer than 1.5
classes per grade and 50% capacity with a projected trend to continue for at least five years.
Heatherwood started the engagement phase last year and is continuing to be monitored.
● Projections indicate that enrollment will continue to decline throughout the district out to the
2029 school year, albeit at a slower rate than last year’s projection.
● In 2029, BVSD is projected to have eight schools at the Enrollment Advisory Phase and three at
the Community Engagement Phase. These schools are distributed with seven in Boulder, one in
Louisville, one in Superior, and two in Broomfield.
● Continued policy revisions provide tools to allow the district to make adjustments to influence
enrollment.
