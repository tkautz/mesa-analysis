Recommended Action

The board of education is asked to adopt the budget for the 2026-27 fiscal year.

DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION

Current state statute specifies that the board of education must be presented with a budget by June 1, and the board must adopt a budget by June 30. The 2026-27 Preliminary Budget was presented to the board of education at the regular meeting on April 14, 2026, and the 2026-27 Proposed Budget on May 26, 2026. The 2026-27 Proposed Budget document is located on the BVSD Financial Transparency website at 
https://www.bvsd.org/about/financial-transparency/budget
.

After adopting a budget by June 30, 2026, the board of education may consider and adopt final revisions to the budget prior to January 31, 2027, in accordance with state law. The Budget Resolution (No. 26-25), Appropriation Resolution (No. 26-19), Capital Reserve Projects Resolution (No. 26-20), and the Use of Beginning Fund Balance Resolution (No. 26-21) are attached. 

Our adopted rules of Parliamentary Procedure, Robert's Rules, provide for a consent agenda listing several items for approval of the Board by a single motion.  Items may be removed from the consent agenda at the request of any board member. 

Motion & Voting

The Board is asked to approve Consent Grouping.

Motion by Ana Temu Otting, second by Jorge Chavez.

Final Resolution: Motion Passes

Yes: Deann Bucher, Jorge Chavez, Alex Medler, Lalenia Quinlan Aweida, Nicole Rajpal, Ana Temu Otting
