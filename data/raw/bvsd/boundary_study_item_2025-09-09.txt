Boulder
Crest View east of Diagonal to Heatherwood
Primary Objectives:
● Balance resident population across schools to maintain high-quality
learning experiences for students
When enrollment is uneven across schools, it impacts funding and the
ability to provide essential programs and staff, such as classroom
teachers, counselors, and specialists in art, music, P.E., and libraries.
Fewer students can mean fewer adults to support them, which affects
the overall student experience.
● Support community connections
In our planning, we try to keep neighborhoods together as we define
geographic attendance areas on a map. This supports better
transportation planning as well as the sense of community that
develops through shared geography. Social connections grow through
sharing experiences at the places in one’s community such as grocery
stores, parks, restaurants, places of worship, schools, etc.

Family Feedback - Gunbarrel
Crest View to Heatherwood/Centennial to Nevin Platt
● 103 preschool, elementary and middle school students’ families received emails
● 27 parents participated in interviews
Themes/Concerns
● Does this fix the issue?
○ Some families expressed uncertainty about Heatherwood's future. They are concerned about
being reassigned there only to have it close later.
○ A few families commented that BVSD should further investigate the reasons families are
choosing to enroll out of Heatherwood and address those concerns. (Note: In 2024-25,
Heatherwood’s open enrollment out rate was notably lower than Crest View’s)
○ Families are worried about potential impacts on Crest View if boundary changes are
implemented.
● The neighborhood is close and there is a concern that this could fracture it. Students have formed
relationships with peers at school.
10

===== boundary_study_item_2025-09-09.pdf | page 13 of 30 =====
Family Feedback - Gunbarrel
Themes/Concerns (continued)
● Working families commented that they benefit from bus transportation which
would be lost if they choose to stay at their current schools.
● We have also heard via email from families in support of the change from Crest
View to Heatherwood, specifically citing the more bike-friendly commute.
● Parents also noted that students are active in north Boulder sports—moving to
Platt would make it more difficult to participate.
● We also heard that biking to Platt is not feasible for middle schoolers.
● Some families felt the change from Centennial to Platt does not make sense
because of the distance and a more difficult commute.

Boulder
Dual Bear Creek/Creekside to Bear Creek
Primary Objectives:
● Fostering community and neighborhood cohesion
BVSD believes there is value in supporting neighborhood-based
relationships. Assigning all students in this area to Bear Creek supports
neighborhood social connections.
● Aligning with geographic features such as major roads
Broadway is a logical boundary and this change eliminates the necessity to
cross Broadway to access Creekside.
● Maximization of pedestrian access to schools, and efficient use of
transportation
The entire area is within walking distance of Bear Creek and eliminates a
bus stop for Creekside.
