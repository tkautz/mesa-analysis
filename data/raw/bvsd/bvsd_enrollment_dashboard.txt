Welcome to the BVSD Enrollment Dashboard
This dashboard brings together data from multiple sources to illustrate the current status of BVSD’s student
enrollment data, as well as trends over time. This comprehensive set of visualizations has been developed to
build understanding in order to guide decision making and to be transparent with our community about what is
happening in BVSD related to student enrollment.
How to navigate this dashboard
Page Headers:
⬤ Enrollment trend data: Pages with a dark blue header contain data from previous years.
⬤ Current year data: Pages with a medium blue header contain data from the current school year.
Tabs:
Each tab contains multiple data visualizations. To see different visualizations, click on the three horizontal lines in the upper left corner and select from the
options.
Naming Conventions:
School year is denoted by the year in which a school year began. For example, “2025 SY” refers to the school year beginning in Fall 2025 and ending in Spring 2026.

What can I find on this dashboard?
⬤ Data source: monthly
This dashboard includes aggregated enrollment data from the BVSD student information system. These data provide
enrollment records
historical and current snapshots of student enrollments into and out of the district.
Tip: Close this message by clicking on the X in the upper right corner to view reports. Use the drop-down menu in the upper
left to navigate between reports. Click the X on the upper left of the filter menu to close the menu.
Within this group of visualizations you will find:
- Historical Year Over Year: This chart shows enrollment gains and losses for each month of the school year (September -
May) for the past four years.
- Current Year: This visualization shows enrollment gains and losses for each month of the current school year and a net
comparison with the previous three years.
- Student Groups: This chart shows enrollment gains and losses for different groups of students for the current year with a
comparison to the previous three years.
- Withdrawals: This chart shows the four-year trend of reasons given by students for withdrawing from the school district
ranked by frequency.
