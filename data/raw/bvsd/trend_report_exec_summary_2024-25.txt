February 11, 2025
2025 Annual Enrollment Trend Report Executive Summary
Background
In April 2022, the Board created the Long Range Advisory Committee (LRAC) to involve the
community in guiding the district’s response to enrollment decline. In June 2023, the committee
provided the Board with a final report of recommendations . The recommendations included a
process and metrics for when the Board should take action related to the enrollment at a
particular school. In addition, the committee recommended that the Board should receive an
annual enrollment trend report from staff regarding school utilization which would also include
the number of classes per grade level and five-year enrollment projections by school. This is the
second Annual Trend Report
Annual Enrollment Trend Report
Based on the metrics and data presented in the report, particular schools may be identified for
either the Enrollment Advisory Phase or the Community Engagement Phase:
ENROLLMENT ADVISORY PHASE
If an elementary school has 2 or fewer classes per grade level on average and enrollment is
60% of capacity or below , the Board and community should be aware:
● Resource allocation will adhere to the established staffing funding formula to the
greatest extent possible.
● Multi-age classrooms may be necessary to meet the funding formula.
● Library, counseling, and specials may be limited.
COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT PHASE
If an elementary school has 1.5 or fewer classes per grade leve l on average and enrollment is
50% of capacity or below with a projected trend to continue for at least 5 years, the Board will
engage staff to lead a community-based process to develop possible solutions to provide a
thriving, well-resourced educational environment for students and staff. Decision-making should
be in the context of looking at geographic areas or multiple schools if appropriate. Timeline will
be established by the Board.
This process may result in recommendations that could include but are not limited to:

● Focus or specialized programming
● Reallocating grade levels with a neighboring school

Enrollment and Capacity Projections
In December 2024, staff shared the updated annual enrollment data and an analysis of trends.
The Annual Enrollment Trend Report further evaluates this data in terms of each school’s
capacity; what the enrollment count will look like in terms of the number of classes at each
grade level for each school; and 5-year projections for these data points. The report also
identifies what these numbers mean for schools in terms of the LRAC enrollment metrics.
For the 2024-25 school year, there are eight schools that are in the Enrollment Advisory Phase
(Coal Creek, Community Montessori, Eisenhower, Eldorado K-5, Flatirons, Kohl, Mesa, and
Whittier) meaning that they are <=2.0 rounds and <=60% capacity. No schools entered the
Community Engagement Phase (<=1.5 classes per grade and <=50% capacity) this year.
Heatherwood Elementary initiated the Community Engagement Process in the 2023-24 school
year and will continue to be monitored under that designation for several years. Through the
Community Engagement Process, Heatherwood decided to develop a new environmental
STEAM programmatic focus, which will launch in the 2025-26 school year. Heatherwood’s
enrollment did increase slightly this year.
Projections indicate that in the next five years, elementary level enrollment will continue to
decline approximately 1% per year, throughout the district, and by 2029-30, BVSD will have eight
schools in the Enrollment Advisory Phase and three in the Community Engagement Phase.
These schools are distributed with seven in Boulder, one in Louisville, one in Superior, and two in
Broomfield. These projections show some improvement from the previous projections, with two
fewer schools identified overall and three fewer projected to be in the Community Engagement
Phase.
