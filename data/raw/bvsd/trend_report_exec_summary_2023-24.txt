February 2024
2023-24 Annual Enrollment Trend Report Executive Summary
Background
BVSD has been anticipating a gradual decline in enrollment in future years. COVID-related
enrollment loss accelerated this decline dramatically. Declining numbers of students can lead
to budget reductions, inefficient facility operations and most importantly, a less comprehensive
educational experience for students at schools with smaller populations.
In April 2022, the Board created the Long Range Advisory Committee (LRAC) to involve the
community in guiding the district’s response to enrollment decline. In June 2023, the
committee provided the Board with a final report of recommendations . The recommendations
included a process and metrics for when the Board should take action related to the enrollment
at a particular school. In addition, the committee recommended that, beginning in February
2024, the Board should receive an annual enrollment trend report from staff regarding school
utilization which would also include the number of classes per grade level and five-year
enrollment projections by school.
Annual Enrollment Trend Report
Based on the metrics and data presented in the report, particular schools may be identified for
either the Enrollment Advisory Phase or the Community Engagement Phase:
ENROLLMENT ADVISORY PHASE
If an elementary school has fewer than two classes per grade level and enrollment is less than
60% of capacity , the Board and community should be aware:
● Resource allocation will adhere to the established staffing funding formula to the
greatest extent possible.
● Multi-age classrooms may be necessary to meet the funding formula.
● Library, counseling, and specials may be limited.
COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT PHASE
If an elementary school’s enrollment continues to decline below 50% capacity and fewer than
1.5 average classes per grade leve l with a projected trend to continue for at least 5 years, the
Board will engage staff to lead a community-based process to develop possible solutions to

Enrollment and Capacity Projections
In December, staff shared the updated annual enrollment data and an analysis of trends. The
Annual Enrollment Trend Report further evaluates this data in terms of each school’s capacity;
what the enrollment count will look like in terms of the number of classes at each grade level
for each school; and 5-year projections for these data points. The report also identifies what
these numbers mean for schools in terms of the LRAC enrollment metrics.
For the 2023-24 school year, there are six schools that are in the Enrollment Advisory Phase
(Coal Creek, Community Montessori, Eldorado K-5, Flatirons, Kohl, and Mesa) meaning that
they are <= 2.0 rounds or are at <= 60% capacity. One school, Heatherwood, is in the
Community Engagement Phase, meaning it is at <=1.5 classes per grade and <=50% capacity.
Projections indicate that in the next five years, enrollment will continue to decline throughout
the district, and by 2028-29, BVSD will have seven schools at the Enrollment Advisory Phase
and six at the Community Engagement Phase. These schools are distributed with nine in
Boulder, one in Louisville, one in Superior, and two in Broomfield.
Community Engagement Phase for Heatherwood
Since Heatherwood has met the metrics for the Community Engagement Phase, staff is
planning to launch a Community Engagement Process at the beginning of March. Details of the
process will be shared at the Feb. 27 Board meeting.
