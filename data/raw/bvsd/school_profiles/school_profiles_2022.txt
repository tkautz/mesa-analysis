BVSD TRENDS AT A GLANCE
ENROLLMENT SUMMARY
BVSD enrollment was down 565 students in 2022 and is still experiencing lingering effects from the
COVID-19 pandemic. Even before the pandemic, growth had been leveling and entering a period of
gradual decline. The district's neighborhood population (students living within the BVSD boundary) are
also experiencing a similar decline while the number of out-of-district students has been level.
