ENROLLMENT SUMMARY
BVSD enrollment was down -525 students in 2025 and appears to be settling into a trend of
overall decline. The district's neighborhood population (students living within the BVSD
boundary) is also experiencing a similar decline while the number of out-of-district students
increased significantly in 2023, 2024, and again in 2025.
