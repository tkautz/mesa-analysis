ENROLLMENT SUMMARY
BVSD enrollment was down 279 students in 2021 and continues to see losses related to the COVID-19
pandemic. Prior to this year, growth had been leveling and entering a period of gradual decline. The
district's neighborhood population (students living within the BVSD boundary) as well as students
from outside the district are also experiencing a similar decline.
