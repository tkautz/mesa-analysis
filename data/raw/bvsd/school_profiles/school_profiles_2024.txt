BVSD TRENDS AT A GLANCE
ENROLLMENT SUMMARY
BVSD enrollment was down -141 students in 2024 and appears to be settling into a trend of
overall decline following the disruption of the COVID-19 pandemic. The district's
neighborhood population (students living within the BVSD boundary) is also experiencing a
similar decline while the number of out-of-district students increased significantly in 2023
and again in 2024.
