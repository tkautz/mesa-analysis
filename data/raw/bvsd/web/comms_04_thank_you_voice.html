    Thank you for sharing your voice

     

    Thank you to the many parents, staff and community members who participated in our six Connect with BVSD: Resilient Schools Community Engagement Sessions in April.

     

    The most meaningful part was seeing participants coming together to discuss their values, priorities and other considerations for BVSD’s solutions to address declining enrollment.

     

    It is not too late to participate

    BVSD sent the Resilient Schools Community Survey to staff on April 21 and families on April 28, giving everyone an opportunity to participate.  

     

    While the survey does not provide the same opportunity for learning and dialogue as the in-person sessions, it gives everyone a chance to share their values and priorities before we conclude this phase of engagement.

     

    The final day to complete the survey is Sunday, May 17.

     

    The survey link sent via email is unique and may only be used once. If you have not already completed the survey, another reminder and unique link will be sent on May 15.

     

    All survey results and feedback from the sessions will be presented to the Board of Education during a report scheduled for Tuesday, June 9.
