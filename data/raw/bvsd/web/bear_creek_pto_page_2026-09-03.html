Bear Creek PTO

Every student's success is rooted in support from home.​

Thank you for your interest in the Bear Creek Parent-Teacher Organization! Our mission is to support our students and enhance their learning experience. We collaborate with the staff and teachers at Bear Creek Elementary to sustain our school's exemplary educational performance, provide resources, and create experiences to bring our community together. The Bear Creek PTO Board is comprised of a group of parents elected to represent our parent community. The PTO Board meets on the second Tuesday of every month in the Fishbowl and the times will be listed on the Bear Creek calendar.  All parents are welcome to join in our monthly meetings! For more information regarding the Bear Creek PTO, or to contact a board member, please visit the Bear Creek PTO  website.
