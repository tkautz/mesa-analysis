A time to rest, reflect and prepare for what comes next

Dear BVSD Community,

We hope you are enjoying Summer Break.

We want to once again express our gratitude to everyone who participated in the Resilient Schools engagement process. Hundreds of community members joined us at engagement sessions, and thousands more shared their perspectives through the digital survey. Thank you for taking the time to engage in this important conversation.

From the beginning, our commitment was to listen before taking action. The values, priorities and perspectives shared by our community will help guide the difficult decisions ahead, as we consider how best to position BVSD for the future.

One message was clear: our community understands that declining enrollment presents real challenges and that maintaining high-quality educational experiences for students may require change. We also heard a strong desire to preserve what makes BVSD special—excellent teaching and learning, strong school communities, and opportunities for every student to thrive.

Understanding the options

The Board has identified several different types of adjustments that could be made to schools to respond to declining enrollment and use resources more efficiently. These include introducing, expanding or relocating focus programs; reconfiguring grades between a pair of schools; consolidating two schools into one building and closing one or more schools and redistributing students.
