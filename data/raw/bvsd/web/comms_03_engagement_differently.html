    Why we are approaching engagement differently this time

     

    Dear BVSD Families and Staff,

     

    Beginning next week, we will welcome students, parents, employees and community members to engagement sessions as we look to make our schools resilient in a time of declining enrollment. 

     

    It is crucially important that we hear from our community regarding their values and priorities before we make decisions regarding the future of our schools.

     

    This is markedly different from what happened at the Boulder Valley School District about 20 years ago. Facing a similar decline in enrollment, the decision was made to close a number of schools, and then the district engaged the community.


    This time we are approaching engagement differently. We are committed to listen to as many voices as possible before decisions are made.

    Listening First - Share your perspective 

    What to expect at the community engagement sessions

     

    Community engagement sessions start next week and BVSD elementary and PK-8 families are encouraged to attend. This is your opportunity to learn more about the complexities of how declining enrollment—and the associated reduction in funding—is showing up in our schools,  help us understand your values and priorities and provide your thoughts about the options BVSD is considering.

    We are BVSD parents: Here are our ideas on school consolidation and civic engagement 

     

    This month, we have the opportunity to attend a series of meetings to discuss declining enrollment and the financial pressures facing our schools. Most importantly, we’ll get to share our ideas for what we can do about it. 

     

    Read the full Op Ed from the District Accountability Committee that appeared in the Daily Camera Apr. 8, 2026

     

    Watch for another way to provide input later this month

     

    If you can’t make it to one of the engagement sessions, you’ll have another opportunity to provide input. 

     

    Later this month, all Boulder Valley School District staff and families will be invited to participate in the "Resilient Schools Community Survey." Similar to the engagement sessions, this short survey aims to give our community the opportunity to tell us what matters most as we plan for the future of our school district and strive to maintain the high-quality learning experiences students currently receive. 

     

    Keep an eye on your inbox/text messages for a link to the survey.
