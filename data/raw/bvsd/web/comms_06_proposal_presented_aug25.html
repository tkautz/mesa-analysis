    Resilient Schools proposal presented to Board August 25

     

    This has not been a restful summer for the team here at BVSD.

     

    Our leadership team has worked diligently to evaluate options involving potential school consolidations, closures and reconfigurations. They have analyzed enrollment trends, building capacity, facility conditions and long-term student needs. They weighed the potential challenges of many possibilities and constructively questioned their own thinking.

     

    Now, our team is preparing to present our proposal, reasoning, and next steps of the process to the Board of Education on August 25. We hope you attend or watch. 

     

    As we've shared throughout this process, our goal is to create a more resilient school district that continues to provide an exceptional educational experience despite declining enrollment.

     

    We knew from the beginning there would be no perfect solution. Every option comes with tough challenges, and this work has been every bit as difficult as we expected.

     

    Our team has remained mindful that every proposed change affects students, families and staff members. Those individuals have been at the center of every conversation and every decision.

     

    Every BVSD school is extraordinary because of the people who learn and work there. While buildings are important, our responsibility is to ensure that every student has access to the strongest possible educational experience. Addressing declining enrollment now will help preserve the high-quality instruction, programs and opportunities our students deserve for years to come.

     

    No matter what lies ahead, our commitment remains the same: to work together, support one another and ensure that every student, family and staff member is cared for throughout this transition. They are, and always will be, what matters most.

    Proposal Development

    Developing the Board of Education proposal for school adjustments

     

    Next week, the Board of Education will have its first opportunity to consider and discuss specific school adjustments aimed at making our school district more resilient in this time of declining enrollment. The proposed changes will include school closures, consolidations, and reconfigurations. The worksession will be held Aug. 25 at 3 p.m. at the Education Center and will be broadcast on BVSD’s Education Access station, BV22 (Comcast Channel 22) or online and simulcast on the BVSD Board YouTube channel.

    Before developing a proposal, we listened to our community 


    Closing schools is one of the most difficult tasks a school district can face. We knew it was vitally important to connect with our community to understand their perspectives and priorities before developing specfic changes for the Board to consider. Consistent themes emerged from our extensive community engagement effort last spring: 

    • Smaller class sizes
    • Fewer multi-age classes
    • More time for non-classroom educators and other school professionals to be in schools to support kids

    Understanding the options

     

    The Board has identified several different types of adjustments that could be made to schools to respond to declining enrollment and use resources more efficiently. These include introducing, expanding or relocating focus programs; reconfiguring grades between a pair of schools; consolidating two schools into one building and closing one or more schools and redistributing students.

    Enrollment drops more than expected, Board expresses desire to take action to respond

     

    BVSD’s overall enrollment continued to decline last year, but at a greater rate than expected, BVSD Senior Planner Glen Segrue told the Board in an update in December. The student count dropped 525 students, or 1.9% from last year—1% more than projected. Over the past five years, the downward trend is less steep at 1.3%. This is BVSD’s second biggest drop in enrollment since the pandemic. The district’s enrollment has declined by nearly 3,600 students since 2015.
