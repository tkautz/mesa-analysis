The Proposal
Realizing that enrollment has declined to a point where we do not have the resources in every school to provide the high quality, BVSD education each and every student deserves, last November, the Board directed staff to move toward taking action. The following is the proposal made to the Board on August 25.

WATCH: Resilient Schools Proposal: Brief video covering what you need to know  about the plan presented on August 25.

If approved by the Board of Education on Sept. 22, these changes would take effect beginning with the 2027-28 school year.

The proposal
The plan proposes the closure of Mesa Elementary and merging of the school communities in the Bear Creek building. The attendance area boundaries for both schools would be redrawn to create a single attendance area, with addresses in the consolidated area now assigned to the Bear Creek building.

Maps showing proposed boundary changes for Mesa

How we arrived at this proposal

While many schools in the South Boulder area have been impacted by declining enrollment, Mesa is currently one of the lowest-enrolled schools, and projections show its numbers are expected to continue to decline. Additionally, Mesa, which currently uses about half of its capacity, also has the second-lowest number of resident students in its attendance area.

Bear Creek, located less than a mile away, has a larger building capacity and can house the projected enrollment of both schools in 2027-28 and beyond. 

Closing Mesa reduces excess capacity in South Boulder. Combining the attendance areas provides the resident student population for a three-round school at Bear Creek.
Bringing more students into Bear Creek will help grow and sustain the staffing, programs, and opportunities that create a high-quality student experience.

Additional Considerations
All existing AIM and RISE programs would be housed at Bear Creek.
Transportation would continue to be provided based on BVSD policy, and as required by a student’s IEP.
Preschool will continue to be offered in the area. More information about specific program locations will be provided by October 1.
School Age Care (SAC) will be offered at Bear Creek.
