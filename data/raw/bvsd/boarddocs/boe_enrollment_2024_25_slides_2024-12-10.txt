2024 Enrollment Trend
Summary
● Enrollment declined by -141/-0.5% over last year, but was better than expected.
● Elementary and high school levels saw declines in 2024. Middle level saw a second
year of surprising gains. Kindergarten (1,609) was the largest since the pandemic.
● Stronger resident student gains at middle school may still be be from students
re-entering the system after the pandemic.
● Out of District contributions to enrollment in 2024 was close to last year’s historic
high.
● Boulder, Louisville, and Superior largely driving enrollment declines, but
Lafayette/Erie showed modest growth in 2024.
● Births showing signs of leveling after a period of steep declines in Boulder.
● Stronger student “net migration” in 2024.
