BVSD Enrollment Update 2023 14
Summary
Summary
● Enrollment declined by -249/-0.9% over last year, but was better than expected.
● Elementary (esp. kindergarten) and high school levels saw declines in 2023. Middle level
showed a surprising gain.
● Stronger resident student gains at middle school and 9th may be students re-entering the
system after the pandemic.
● Out of District contributions to enrollment reached a historic high in 2023.
● Boulder, Louisville, and Superior largely driving enrollment declines, but typical offset from
new housing growth in Lafayette/Erie not present 2022 or 2023.
● Births showing signs of leveling after a period of steep declines in Boulder.
● Stronger student “net migration” in 2023 and improvements in regional indicators.
