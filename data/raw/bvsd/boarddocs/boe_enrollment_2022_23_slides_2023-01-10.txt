Summary
● Greater than expected enrollment decline (-565/-2%) over last year.
● Losses concentrated in Kindergarten and 1st grade.
● Elementary, middle, and high school levels all saw declines in 2022.
● Boulder, Louisville, and Superior schools largely driving declines, but typical offset
from new housing growth in Lafayette and Erie not seen in 2022.
● Out of Districts contributions to enrollment down from 2016 peak, but stable since
2019.
● Future enrollment levels largely determined by birth rates and net migration. Signs
of faltering net migration in 2022.
