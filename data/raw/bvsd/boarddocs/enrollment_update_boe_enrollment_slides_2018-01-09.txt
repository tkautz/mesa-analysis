SUMMARY of Growth Components
• +
Enrollment Growth is Positive (141/.5%) but slowing; significantly
negative at Elementary (-171/-1.4%).
• +
Resident Student Growth also Positive but slowing; loss even greater
at Elementary (-227/1.8%).
• -
Losses at Elementary in all communities except Lafayette-Erie, which
saw strong resident Elementary growth (+208/9.9%).
• + The influence of cohort (grade-levels) size was strong in 2017 with a
small outgoing 12th grade driving overall growth. A very large outgoing
5th also drove elementary decline.
• +/- The number of overall Out-of-District students was level this year,
new O.O.D. was average (even with the opening of Meadowlark).
• + “Net Migration” positive and on par with 2016, but below other recent
years
• -
Kindergarten negative, with strong recovery at 1st. Classes still small
and cumulatively creating a drag at elementary.
