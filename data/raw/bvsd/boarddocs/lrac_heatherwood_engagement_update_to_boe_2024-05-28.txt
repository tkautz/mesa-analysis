LRAC/Heatherwood Community Engagement Update 6
Heatherwood Educational Advisory Team
Purpose
The Heatherwood Educational Advisory Team will develop recommendations
regarding future programming and educational experiences for the school in
response to declining enrollment. The Team is intended to provide a high level of
accountability, involvement, and communication with the Heatherwood and Boulder
Valley School District communities to ensure stakeholders are engaged and their
values and priorities are reflected in decision-making. The Team is advisory in
nature and approval for programmatic changes remains with District staff and the
Board of Education.
