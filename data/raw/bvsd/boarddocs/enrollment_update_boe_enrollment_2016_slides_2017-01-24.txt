SUMMARY
• Primary factors for 2016 loss:
• Large outgoing 12th grade class
• Weak in-migration among BVSD’s resident students concentrated
at the high school level
• Other factors influencing negative growth:
• Fewer than anticipated housing units built in 2016 (project delays).
• Out-of-District population grew at a lower rate than expected; had
fewer new applicants.
• Low inventory in the existing housing market.
• Regional enrollment growth down with other districts
• In-coming resident kindergarteners low, but on par with recent years.
• Positive Growth next year (2017) more likely with:
• Small departing 12th grade class
• A significant increase in the correlating births from 2011-12
• A larger number of new houses coming on-line.
• 2018 forward will have more limited growth potential as class sizes
stabilize across all grade levels.
