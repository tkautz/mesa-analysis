Environmental STEAM: Inspired by Nature
Program Description
● STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) principles
through a focus on environmental literacy.
● Fosters a deep connection between students and the natural world, encouraging
them to love, explore, understand, and creatively address environmental
challenges through a multidisciplinary approach.
● Boulder County Environmental Education Guidelines (E-Guidelines) to learning
connects students to their academic and social learning.
● Aligned with Seal of Climate Literacy
● Future development of elementary-HS Environmental STEAM pathway
● Colorado content standards and academic rigor will continue to be the core of
the academic program at Heatherwood

Environmental STEAM: Inspired by Nature
Expected Outcomes
Students show growth over time in four areas of their learning as laid out in
Boulder County Environmental Education Guidelines (E-Guidelines)
● Academic skills and knowledge (Head)
● Social-emotional learning (Heart)
● Action and service (Hands)
● Connection to place (Feet)
