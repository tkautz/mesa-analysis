Colorado School Choice Law
● Since 1990, Colorado law has provided the right for students to seek
enrollment in particular programs or schools within their school districts
and for nonresident students to apply in particular programs or schools
within such school district without paying tuition. C.R.S. § 22-36-101
● Subject to available space, teaching staff and program availability

Open Enrollment Policy Worksession 3
Key Provisions of BVSD’s JECC & JECC-R
● Centralized OE application lottery process
● District establishes seven district-wide preferences; seven
non-charter schools have special preferences; three charter
schools have special preferences
● Upon open enrollment, the school becomes the “school of
attendance” through the highest grade offered at the school.
● OE enrollment does not include transportation
● Students with Disabilities have the same opportunity to open
enroll as their typical peers. If a lottery seat is given, then the
School District will convene as necessary an IEP team to
determine whether the selected school can deliver FAPE

Open Enrollment Policy Worksession 66
Trends Over Time
● With declining enrollment, families now have a much higher likelihood
of being offered space at a school they applied to
● Last year, most schools’ wait lists were “wiped clean”
● For the last 2 years, 1st choice seats were offered to all in-district
families with on-time OE applications, except for charter schools and
the few schools with space or programmatic limitations
● Many schools that used to experience long waitlists either have no
waitlists now or are able to accommodate all students who apply
through OE

Open Enrollment Policy Worksession 20
BVSD Policy JECC-R Open Enrollment
It has been practice to update this regulation
annually.
In November 2022, BVSD updated this regulation
to simplify the language, to reflect changes in the
law (such as the State's universal preschool
program and changes to the Exceptional Children's
Education Act Rules), and to incorporate changes
necessitated by the school district's new
enrollment software system.
