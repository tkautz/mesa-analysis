Enrollment 2020
Summary
Summary
• Steep enrollment decline (-1,616/-5%) due largely to pandemic.
• Pre-pandemic expectation was decline closer to -226/-0.8%.
• Losses concentrated in elementary (-1,149/-10%), especially Kindergarten
(-371/-20%). Middle losses also significant (-441/6.5%).
• High school, charter schools, and out-of-district appear unaffected.
• Losses not only from withdrawals but also from fewer new enrollees.
• Apart from pandemic impacts, overall enrollment patterns of BVSD appear to
still be in slow decline.
