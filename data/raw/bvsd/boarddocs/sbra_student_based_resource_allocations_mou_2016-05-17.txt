Student-Based Resource
Allocations
A Memorandum of Understanding between the Board of Education and the
Boulder Valley Education Association
Purpose
The purpose of this MOU is to establish the parameters of an ongoing work
group charged with using professional judgment and research/experts to
facilitate as needed, in order to determine appropriately differentiated student ­
based allocations of school resources in BVSD; to ensure that all schools have
an equitable playing field, and make recommendations for the allocation of
resources based on the diverse needs of students, classrooms, schools, and
communities across BVSD. This work will support the BVSD Vision, Mission,
Values Goals and the BVSD Strategic Plan.
Vision
Our vision is to create a culture where parents, teachers, staff, students,
administrators, and community members will embrace all of BVSD as one
community. The positive feelings people have for their school will extend to
our district as a whole. We believe that all students should be allocated the
appropriate resources to meet their diverse needs so that they can access the
curriculum and learn through highly effective instruction. The overall goal is to
further student success, with the intention of making them lifelong learners.
Mission
Our mission is to create a system that would be sustainable, fiscally possible,
transparent, flexible, manageable and research-based. We will complete an
analysis of our current allocation model and identify any areas that are not
aligned with our BVSD Vision, Mission, Values and Goals in the Strategic Plan.
With an understanding that equitable is not the same as equal, we will identify
inequity that exists in our district; we will make suggestions for correcting any
misalignments; while avoiding incentivizing the unnecessary labeling of
students, we will create a research-based, basic student allocation framework
to support an equitable playing field.
