Survey: Why Do Parents Not Enroll in BVSD?
*
Key Findings for PK-12 Parents of Non-BVSD Students :
● 68% (214) indicated they may consider BVSD in the future.
● Of the 23% (72) indicating they will not consider BVSD, common reasons
were:
○ Disagreement with District’s ideology and curriculum (46%/33)
○ Concern over school size, safety, and/or culture (17%/12)
○ Seeking a different program for their child’s specific needs (16%/11)
● 9% (28) were unsure whether they will attend BVSD in the future.
*PK-12 respondents; Boulder Valley School District Enrollment Survey, January 2023, 1,120n, +/- 2.92% MoE

Proposed Policy Change
Add to JECC-R a preference for individuals who work within the
boundaries of Boulder Valley School District:
A student of an individual who resides outside of and
works within the boundaries of the Boulder Valley School
District, including Boulder, Broomfield, and Gilpin
counties. Eligible individuals must be working for an
employer in the BVSD boundaries at the time of the
application and on the first day of the applicable school
year.
