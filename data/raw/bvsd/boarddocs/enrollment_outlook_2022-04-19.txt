Enrollment Outlook
Enrollment Big Picture
BVSD has been anticipating a gradual decline in enrollment in future years.
COVID-related enrollment loss accelerated this decline dramatically. Declining
numbers of students can lead to budget reductions, inefficient facility operations
and most importantly, a less comprehensive educational experience for
students at schools with smaller populations.

Impacts of Enrollment Change
11
Budget Impacts
● Each 1% drop in enrollment (300 students) = ~$3M Loss
● Averaging formula in School Finance Act softens the impact of declining
enrollment over multiple years
● Each 1% drop in enrollment = 12 fewer teacher FTE standard staffing
formulas
● Non-linear staffing (administration, operational support, etc.) is slower to
adjust than direct services to students
● Optimal school size to generate economies of scale and improve student
and staff experience
