Summary
● Enrollment declined by -525/-1.9% over last year; 2nd greatest loss since pandemic.
● All levels saw declines in 2025, however kindergarten (1,626) was the largest since
2022.
● Previous student gains at middle school didn’t occur in 2025; may indicate students
are no longer re-entering the system in significant numbers after the pandemic.
● Out of District contributions to enrollment in 2025 were higher than last year’s
historic high.
● Boulder largely driving enrollment declines in 2025, but Lafayette/Erie also showed
declines despite new housing.
● Births showing signs of leveling after a period of steep declines in Boulder.
● Resident student “net migration” faltering in 2025.
