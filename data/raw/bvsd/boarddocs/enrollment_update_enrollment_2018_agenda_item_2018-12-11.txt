PURPOSE:
This report will provide the Board with an update of the October 2018 enrollment count and an
analysis of trends observed.
DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION:
A summary of the District’s enrollment is as follows:
General Fund Only
Elementary down -160 (-1.3%)
Middle down -37 (-.6%)
High up 76 (.8%)
Total K-12 up -121 (-.4%)
Total Enrollment (including Charters)
Elementary down -161 (-1.3%)
Middle down -38 (-.52%)
High up 118 (1.15%)
Total K-12 down -81 (-.27%)
Not counting Preschool or students housed in outside facilities, overall District enrollment
(general fund and charter) is down 81 students in 2018, representing a decline of .3% from last
year. Enrollment as of October 3, 2018 stands at 30,201. This year’s loss represents significantly
lower growth than last year, which posted a gain of .5% (+141). Enrollment change in 2018
represents only the second loss in 13 years, with 2016 also experiencing a similar loss (-64).
Enrollment decline in 2018 was concentrated in the general fund schools which posted a -.4% (-
121) loss and significantly underperforming last year’s gain of .6%. Charter enrollment
experienced a small increase of 40 students, primarily from a rebound in enrollment at Boulder
Preparatory High School and increases at Justice High. Otherwise, charter enrollment has been
level as most available “seats” at these schools have been consistently filled in recent years.
General fund elementary enrollment posted a fourth year of decline, dropping by -160 students,
or -1.3%. This level had experienced a 12 year growth trend peaking in 2010 at 2.4%. However,
growth began to weaken in 2011 and started to trend negative in 2015. The prospects for future
growth at this level have also narrowed for the foreseeable future as cohort size (enrollment at
each grade) has continued to decline. In particular, entering Kindergarten and, to a lesser extent,
1st grade classes have been progressively smaller in recent years.
Kindergarten in 2018 had the smallest enrollment in 14 year at 1,856 students and marked a
significant decrease (-67) over the last year’s class of 1,923. Along with elementary,
kindergarten class size has been trending downward since a peak of 2,113 students in 2011. The
resident kindergartener class (those residing within the district boundary) also declined by a
similar amount (-65).
Middle school general fund enrollment also experienced decline in 2018 losing 37 students from
last year, for a loss of -.6%. Middle school growth was impacted by a smaller incoming 6th grade
class that contributed to the decline. Overall, this grade level has been experiencing a leveling in
class sizes similar to elementary, and is expected to continue to level and possibly decline in the
coming years. Charter enrollment at middle school was on par (-1) with last year’s enrollment.
General fund high school enrollment posted the only gains of all grade levels in 2018 increasing
by 76 students, or .8% over last year. This gain, however, is modest compared to last year’s gain
of 224 students and the previous 5 year average of 139 students. The 2018 gain was assisted by a
somewhat larger incoming 9th grade class and higher in coming out-of-district open enrollment.
As mentioned previously, enrollment expansions at Boulder Preparatory High School and Justice
High helped push up charter high school enrollment by 41 students.
Conclusion
Overall BVSD enrollment change was negative in 2018 declining by 81 students, or -.3% from
last year, primarily in the general fund schools. This contrasts with last year’s gain of 224
students and overall positive growth over the last 13 years. Enrollment in 2016 also posted a
similar loss, however.
Enrollment decline in 2018 was focused primarily at the elementary level with middle school
also declining to a lesser extent. The elementary decline is a continuation of a leveling and
downward trend started in 2015 and was particularly impacted by this year’s very small
kindergarten class. This current kindergarten class is 67 students smaller than in 2017 and is the
smallest class since 2004. The decline at kindergarten is generally consistent with an overall
declining birth rate in BVSD and other Front Range communities, but nonetheless was higher
than expected. Like other recent kindergarten classes, this class is expected to experience a “pop”
in enrollment at first grade next year, but will still likely continue to be a relatively small class as
it matriculates through the system. Middle school has also experienced slowing growth in recent
years as smaller elementary cohorts’ progress to that level, as was the case in 2018.
As stated, high school posted the only enrollment gains in 2018 increasing .8% over last year.
However, further review has shown that high school underperformed growth expectations in at
least one key area. The number of resident high school students leaving BVSD has been
increasing in recent years and peaked in 2018 – a factor that likely constrained high school
growth somewhat this year. Also noteworthy was a small decline in the overall out-of-district
students enrolled at this level.
