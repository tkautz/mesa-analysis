Policy JC
Limitations of Changing Boundaries
● Can only redistribute base resident populations, not overall enrollment
● Other factors affect enrollment too, such as
○ open enrollment
○ program options
○ community values
● Disruptive to families and neighborhoods (e.g. siblings, home purchases)
● Unintended consequences (e.g. community division, transportation impacts)
● Transitional concessions may reduce effectiveness initially
● Not a precise tool for addressing inequities
● Stakeholder dissatisfaction
