Long Range Advisory Committee
LRAC Purpose
To develop recommendations to ensure sound
long-term facility decisions consistent with the needs
of the school district and in support of the school
district’s strategic initiatives
What LRAC IS
● An opportunity to develop metrics and guiding
principles for the board to use in future decision
making related to addressing how declining
enrollment impacts student & staff experiences
and district finances
What LRAC is NOT
● A committee to solve declining enrollment
● A committee to solve affordable housing
● A committee to generate marketing ideas
● A committee to address declining birth rates

Long Range Advisory Committee 8
Financial Impacts
● As enrollment declines at a school, fixed costs are distributed over
fewer students, increasing the cost per student.
● Instructional resources may also ratchet down, corresponding to lower
enrollment.
● Disproportionate resources are required to maintain smaller schools,
diverting funding from other student and district needs.
● As the number of smaller schools increases, the proportion of the
general fund allocated to subsidize those schools grows.
● The school district’s budget is about trade-offs; money spent in one
area cannot be spent elsewhere.
