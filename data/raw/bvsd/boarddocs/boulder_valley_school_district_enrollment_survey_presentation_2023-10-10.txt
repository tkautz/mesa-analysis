Methodology
Magellan Strategies are pleased to present the
results of an online survey of 1,995 parents of
children in 5th Grade or younger within the Boulder
Valley School District boundaries. The interviews
were conducted from September 5th – 19th, 2023 and
the survey was available in English and Spanish.
The overall survey responses have a margin of error
of +/- 2.18% at the 95% confidence interval.
Population subgroups will have a higher margin of
error than the overall sample.
