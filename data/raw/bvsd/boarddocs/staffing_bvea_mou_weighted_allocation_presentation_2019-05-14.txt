WEIGHTED STUDENT ALLOCATIONS 5
2018-19 BVEA Contract MOU
PURPOSE
Create a workgroup, established by May
18, 2018, to develop a consistent,
district-wide formula for distributing
weighted student staffing based on
specific student characteristics that were
previously recommended by the MOU on
Student-Based Resource Allocation
(SBRA-Fall 2016) and aligned with the
proposed Colorado Modernized Student
Finance Act (MSFA).
WEIGHTED STUDENT ALLOCATIONS 6
2018-19 BVEA Contract MOU
CHARGE
Recognizing that students have diverse learning needs this MOU is
intended to create a work group that would address access to
resources.
WEIGHTED STUDENT ALLOCATIONS 7
2018-19 BVEA Contract MOU
This workgroup will consider factors such as:
● The “Ideal School” (minimum resources and programs that a
school needs to be successful).
● Consistency between schools to define and identify students
who receive weighted resources (staffing).
● Alternative characteristics such as frequent behavior referrals,
excessive absences, and social/emotional needs that may be
used to consider a student as exhibiting high needs.
● The impact of all available resources on student learning.
WEIGHTED STUDENT ALLOCATIONS 8
2018-19 BVEA Contract MOU
The workgroup will:
● Recommend actual student factors and the weight of these
factors to be considered in the allocation formula.
● Recommend criteria for evaluating the effectiveness of
weighted resource allocations that coincide with the district
UIP goals.
● This committee would make a recommendation to inform the
ongoing district budgeting process.
