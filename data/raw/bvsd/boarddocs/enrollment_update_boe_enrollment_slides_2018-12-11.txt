SUMMARY of Growth Components
-
Overall enrollment change is negative (-81/-0.3%); even more so at Elementary
(-161/-1.3%).
-
Resident student change also negative (-48/-0.2%)
-
Resident losses at Elementary concentrated in Boulder, Broomfield, and Superior with
Lafayette/Erie experiencing the strongest Elementary growth (+72/3.1%).
-
The influence of cohort (grade-levels) size was strong in 2018 with a small incoming
Kindergarten (-63/-0.3%) contributing to enrollment decline, especially at the Elementary
level.
-
The number of overall Out-of-District students was down in 2018 (-41), although new
O.O.D. was above average.
+
“Net Migration” positive and on par with 2017, but below other recent years
