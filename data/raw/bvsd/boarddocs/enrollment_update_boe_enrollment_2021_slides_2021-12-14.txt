Summary
● Continued enrollment decline (-279/-1%) over last year.

● Middle-level appears to have gotten fewer students back from 2020.

● Boulder schools largely driving declines, but offset somewhat by new housing
growth from Lafayette and Erie.
● Future enrollment levels largely determined by birth rates and net migration.
