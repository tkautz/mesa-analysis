Introducing the BVSD App
BVSD is now using Apptegy, an app-based messaging tool that all families can download to communicate with schools, their
teachers, and the district.
All schools, all in one place for BVSD families. The app features a live feed of news and updates, the ability to receive push
notifications, and two-way communication between teachers, coaches and parents.
