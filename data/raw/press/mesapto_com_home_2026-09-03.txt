Welcome to Mesa Elementary PTO
Thank you for supporting students, staff and teachers at Mesa Elementary in Boulder, Colorado. 
The Mesa Elementary Parent Teacher Organization (PTO) has been an active part of the school for nearly 50 years.  
As a Mesa family, you are automatically a part of the PTO; there are no fees to join or a membership.

The PTO supports the administration to plan and fund activities which improve students' academic experience and serve to build and enrich the community at Mesa. The PTO meets the first Friday of each month and all are welcome to join! 
​We are always looking for parents and caregivers to help out in big and small ways, either from home or at school. Volunteers are needed for events, fundraisers, and more throughout the year.
